The problem

The work is not hard. Keeping track of it is.

A single home football Saturday can carry a hundred contracted obligations, a hundred and fifty staff shifts and a dozen vendors on load-in windows that overlap. None of it is complicated on its own. All of it at once, across every sport, in spreadsheets and texts, is how things get missed.

01

Obligations slip quietly

A missed PA read or video board spot rarely gets noticed until a partner asks for a recap at renewal time.

02

Staffing runs on memory

Who works which gate, who confirmed, who no-showed last time — usually known by one person, stored nowhere.

03

Nothing carries forward

When a coordinator leaves, the run-of-show and the vendor relationships tend to leave with them.

Modules

What’s inside.

Start with the piece that hurts most. Add the rest when you are ready — everything shares the same calendar, the same venues and the same people.

Sponsorship inventory

Every asset you sell — signage, PA reads, video board, digital, hospitality — catalogued once, with its own availability, rate and owner.

Obligation tracking

Contracts become a checklist of deliverables mapped to specific dates and events, so a missed read is caught the same week, not at renewal.

Proof of performance

Capture the evidence as it happens — timestamps, photos, as-run logs — and generate the recap a partner will actually renew against.

Event staffing

Build shift templates per sport and venue, publish openings, assign crews, and track who confirmed, who checked in and who is short.

Run of show

One shared timeline per event: load-in, rehearsal, promos, activations, media timeouts. Everyone works from the same document, live.

Vendors & venues

Keep vendor contacts, load-in windows, access requirements and venue-specific rules attached to the event instead of buried in email.

Multi-sport scheduling

See the whole athletic calendar at once — overlapping events, shared staff, shared spaces — and catch conflicts before they are a Saturday problem.

Reporting

Season-level views of fulfillment rate, staffing coverage and cost, ready to hand to a director, a partner or a board.
