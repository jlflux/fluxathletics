About

We build for the people off the field

Flux Athletics creates software, tools and strategy for the side of sports the crowd never sees - the operations, partnerships and production work that makes game day smooth.

The company

One company,
multiple fronts.

We stared from a simple observation: most athletic departments are severely understaffed and typically rely on volunteers, students and parents to help make game day happen.

So we continue to work tirelessly to create solutions, including our Athletics Command Center for sponsorship and gameday logistics, broadcast toolkits for those that can't be in the stadium or arena, and consulting for decisions that software can't make for you.

What we believe

Four things

01

Operations

The game is the visible part. For most athletic departments, winning on the field is only part of the equation for what makes a successful season.

02

Tools to fit the crew you have

Nobody has the amount of staff members they want. Everything we build assumes fewer people, not more.

03

Prove it or do not sell it

A sponsorship is worth what you can demonstrate you delivered. We build for evidence, because renewals are won with it.

04

Simple beats complete

A system people actually use every day is work more than a complicated one they abandon within a few months.
